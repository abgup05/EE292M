## What checking this one turned up

Finding 8 said the fix for Costco needed a test on Costco. Writing it led to a command, `make figures`, that prints each company's quarters as a run sees them, so that a change to how periods are placed can be seen on all eight probe companies and not one. Run before and after the fix, only Costco's lines moved. On its first run it also showed two faults nobody had reported:

- **Amazon's fiscal years were not fiscal years.** Its quarterly reports tag cash flows for the twelve months ended each quarter. Those periods are as long as a year, so the two newest "fiscal years" ended on 30 September and 30 June 2026, held a cash flow and nothing else, and pushed the real fiscal years out of the figures a run offers. A year-long period is now a fiscal year only when an annual report reported it. Amazon went from 26 figures offered to 38.
- **JPMorgan had no revenue.** A bank tags its total net revenue under a concept the revenue group did not read. It went from 11 figures offered to 20.

Both are in `docs/evidence/figures-eight-companies.md` with the lines that moved. Both are the mistake this project's notes already name, testing on one company, and both had been in the tool since version 3.

The fix to finding 8 had a cost that was paid, not hidden: Costco's figures changed, so the recorded answers for the five Costco cases of the golden set no longer matched their prompts. They were recorded again, with the 16 planted-mismatch requests that held a Costco finding. Verdict agreement stayed at 20 of 24, with one Costco case moving each way.

## What this exercise showed

Three referees have now read three versions, and every one of them found sentences in the project's own documents that claimed more than the code did; a column heading, the word "complete" and "two words of the claim" are three of them. No test had failed on any, because the tests check the code against itself. The finding that mattered most this time was one the referee could only reach by reading: a period length chosen on the first three companies and never tried on the fourth.
