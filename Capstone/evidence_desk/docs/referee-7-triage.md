# The seventh referee, finding by finding (version 14.2's analysis)

The referee read commit `5ef601d` (the code of tag `capstone-v14.2`) with read-only tools and ran nothing ([its text, unedited](reviews/referee-7-version-14.2.md)). Its brief had been extended to name the claims of versions 12 to 14.2, so this is the first hostile reading of the analysis itself. It could not read the public site's guard, which lives outside the directories it was given, and says so; nothing here answers that part.

What was done, on branch `capstone/fixes-14-3` (19 November 2026): for each finding a test was written from the referee's own description, run, and seen to fail or not; then the code was changed where the finding was real; then the recordings and baselines were regenerated and the differences read. Tag `capstone-v14.2` is as it was. Three of the referee's statements did not hold when run, and each row says which. Finding 4 is the owner's to decide and nothing was changed for it.

## What testing them showed

Nine of the twelve held as described. Three statements did not. A second root of the reverse DCF cannot exist; the proof is short and is now in the module's notes. No sentence says the calculation reproduces the market value. And a record the live app cannot read does not pass the restore check. The referee marked each of its inputs as untested, so these are corrections, not complaints.

Looking where the referee pointed found three faults it had not named: the reverse DCF's direction note used the wrong line; a 13F price could go unconfirmed because of the order the managers are read in; and a year-exemption rule written to answer finding 1 would, in its first form, have marked two true sentences of the Costco sample ("Costco's 2025 growth", "peers' 2026 figures"), which `make audit-recount` showed before anything was committed.

One constraint shaped two fixes. Text an analytic shows the analysing model is part of the recorded requests: a changed word in a reading leaves the recorded answers of both examples unplayable, and with no key in this work they could not be recorded again. So the reverse DCF's usual wording and two loose sentences were left as they were, and the documents say what they mean.
