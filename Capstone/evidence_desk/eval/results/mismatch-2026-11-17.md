## How the mismatches were built

76 verified findings with at least one quote (2 without a quote were left out). Each keeps its claim, statement and stance; its quotes are replaced by:

- `other-company`: the quotes of a verified finding of a case about a different company.
- `other-claim`: the quotes of a verified finding of a different case about the same company.

The verified findings with at least one quote are sorted by (case id, position of the finding in its case). For the finding at place k of that order, the candidate donors of a kind are the findings, in the same order, of every case about a different company (other-company) or of every other case about the same company (other-claim). The donor is the first candidate, starting at place k modulo the number of candidates and wrapping round, that shares no quote with the finding: no whitespace-normalised quote of one equals, contains or is contained in a quote of the other. A finding with no candidate, or whose every candidate shares a quote, is skipped and counted. No randomness and no seed.

Cited figures are dropped from every mismatched item, so its judgement rests on the swapped quotes alone; the true pairs were judged with their figures.

## What it does not show

- Swapped quotes are an easy kind of mismatch: they are usually about another topic, and for other-company about another business, whose name or products may appear in them. Catching them says little about the hard case, a quote on the right topic that supports only a weaker or a different statement.
- The answers come from whoever answered the cassette, named above from its envelopes (`via`, `model`). An answer written by a stand-in is not the model a run calls, and a stand-in that knows it is reading planted mismatches is not a blind reader.
- Each request holds only mismatched items (one case, one kind), whereas a run shows the checker a claim's findings together, most of them true; a reader may judge a batch of mismatches differently from one mismatch among true pairs.
- One recording per request: no variance across samples, and no test of the live API.
- Cited figures are dropped from every mismatched item, so its judgement rests on the swapped quotes alone; the true pairs were judged with their figures.
