      How it works

      1. Write it down. A ticker, the decision you face, and the claims your thesis rests on.
      2. Read the record. The latest annual report (10-K), the quarterly reports (10-Q) after it, the current reports (8-K) since the annual report, and the figures the company tagged (XBRL); then the passages that bear on each claim.
      3. Check every quote. An engine proposes findings, each citing quotes or figures. Every quote is checked word for word against the stored filing and every number against its quotes and figures; a finding that fails is withheld.
      4. Decide for yourself. Each verdict follows by a fixed rule from the findings that passed. Whether a quote supports a claim is the engine's reading: open the claim and read the quote before you rely on it.

      How to read these tables
      What this is
      The numbers one filing tags in its inline XBRL, split by a dimension (segment, product, geography, customer): read from the tags, not from the printed tables. Every cell and change is the record's own text; this page computes none of them.
      ƒFixed rule
      Computed by a fixed rule; not checked by a person. Here: which tags make a table, the row labels (derived from tag names, not the company's own), the changes and whether the rows add up.
      = Adds up
      The rows add up to the total the filing tags, in every period checked, within the rounding the tags declare. The table's notes say how (some rows can be parts of others).
      ▲ Does not add up
      In at least one period the rows do not add up to the tagged total: a limit of the record. Read the filing's table before you use the rows.
      Not checked
      Nothing could be checked: the filing tags no total for these rows (shares of revenue by customer, say), or no period had both.
      empty cell
      The filing tags no value for that row in that period. It is not a zero.
      +68.2%
      The change from the comparable earlier period, as the record states it. Open it to read the arithmetic, or why there is none.
      −4.1%
      A negative number, as the record writes it, is set in its own colour; its minus sign is always there too.

      Something limits this record.

                      How to read a run

                      Each verdict follows by a fixed rule from the findings that passed their checks. Checked: every quote was found word for word in the stored filing, and numbers were matched to quotes and reported figures. Not checked: whether a quote supports or challenges the claim is the engine's reading of it. Read the quotes before you rely on a verdict.

                      The tape under the company's name is the newest period's reported figures, each with the line of its series. The time line puts the run on one axis of dates: bars above it are the annual (10-K) and quarterly (10-Q) reports the run read, with a dot where a verified quote comes from one; below it are the 8-Ks the record lists since the annual report, filled diamonds for those with an item of a substantive category and small circles for routine ones; the hatched band runs from the newest filing read to the run, and nothing filed in it is in the record. Hover over or focus a mark to read it; select it to open the filing.

                      The page's marks are explained in the legend at the end of the note. Keys: / ticker, e your thesis, i the inbox, j k claims, t theme, ? all keys. Printing gives the whole note.

                      The marks on this page
                      ✓Supported
                      Verified findings support the claim and none challenge it. On a finding, the same mark means it supports the claim.
                      ✕Challenged
                      Verified findings challenge the claim and none support it. On a finding: it challenges the claim.
                      ±Mixed
                      Verified findings point both ways.
                      ?Not judged
                      Passages were quoted but no engine judged them.
                      ∅Not addressed
                      No verified quote or figure bears on the claim.
                      •Context
                      A finding that informs the claim without taking a side.
                      ▲ Limit
                      Something limits this record: a partial or failed run, a coverage limit, an error, a source not read, a failed check.
                      ✎ Model-written
                      Written by a language model and not checked for meaning by a person. A second model's reading has its own marks, next.
                      ◎Agrees
                      A second model, not shown the first one's reasoning, read the finding's evidence and agrees that it supports the statement and its stance. Still a model's reading, not a person's: its reason is one click away.
                      ◐In part
                      The second model agrees only in part; its reason is shown under a double violet rule. A result of the check, not a limit of the record: read the quote and decide.
                      ▲ Period not found
                      A year a verified finding names that is not in its quotes, its cited figures or the period of a filing it quotes. It is reported only: it withholds nothing and changes no verdict. A year one below the filing's own is often the comparison year a report prints beside its own; read the quote to see which year it means.
                      ▲ Predates an 8-K
                      Every filing a finding cites is older than an 8-K whose item bears on its claim's subject. A claim whose verdict rests on such a finding carries a caution. Reported only: no verdict changes.
                      ! Refused
                      The server refused your input; the message says how to fix it.
                      dashed border
                      Not final. A dashed border marks one of three things: a claim no engine judged (Not judged); a decision you have still to make (Undecided, not reviewed yet); a run still in progress, with its claims not yet decided.
                      ƒFixed rule +Added −Removed ~Reworded #Numbers only
                      Under each claim of a finished run: paragraphs that changed between the newest annual and the newest quarterly report the run read and the filing of the same form filed before each (fetched for the comparison; the run may not have read it), and that share words with the claim or the search queries the run wrote for it. Listed by a fixed rule (ƒ: computed by a fixed rule; not checked by a person); no model chose or judged them, they are not findings, and none counts toward the verdict. In a reworded pair, struck through was dropped and underlined was added.
                      ◇ Complete
                      The run's status, at the top of a result: an open diamond when complete, three dots while running, the limit mark when partial or failed. A status is never a filled tag.
                      Time line
                      One axis of dates from the record: tall bars are the annual and quarterly reports the run read (a dot above one means a quote comes from it); filled diamonds are 8-Ks with an item of a substantive category, small circles routine ones; the hatched band is the time between the newest filing read and the run. Hover over or focus a mark to read it and open the filing.

                      Terms used on this page
                      10-K
                      The annual report a US public company files with the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC).
                      10-Q
                      The quarterly report, filed for each of the other three quarters.
                      8-K
                      A current report, filed within days of an event such as a change of officers, an acquisition or a quarter's results.
                      XBRL
                      The machine-readable tags in a filing that state each reported number, its period and its unit. Reported figures on this page are read from them.
                      Item
                      A numbered section of a filing, such as Item 1A, Risk Factors, or Item 7, Management's Discussion and Analysis (MD&A).
                      Period end
                      The last day of the quarter or fiscal year the filing reports on. Fiscal years need not match calendar years.
                      Filed
                      The date the company submitted the filing to the SEC. "Retrieved" is when this run downloaded it, in UTC.
                      Accession number
                      The SEC's identifier for one filing; hover over a form to see it.
                      Derived, restated
                      A derived value was worked out from reported ones (for example a fourth quarter as the year less nine months). A restated value was reported differently by a later filing.
                      CIK
                      The SEC's numeric identifier for a company.
                      EDGAR
                      The SEC's public filing system, where the filings linked on this page are published.
