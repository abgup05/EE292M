## What I built

I built an interactive visualization of how U.S. industries purchase goods and services from different product categories. The app uses the Bureau of Economic Analysis's annual Supply–Use tables, covering 1997–2025, with 71 buying industries and 73 product categories.

The main question is **“Who buys what?”** Users can explore it in two directions: select an industry to see its largest production inputs, or select a product category to see which industries buy it. Ranked bars show purchase amounts and shares of the relevant total. Users can compare two years, rank relationships by gains in share, and click a relationship to see its annual history in dollars or as a share of total purchases. Exact annual values and a CSV download are also available.

For example, the app shows that construction accounted for 36.0% of industrial purchases of electrical equipment, appliances, and components in 2025, up 3.2 percentage points from 2019.

## Why this is useful

The idea connects to my interests in equity research. Understanding an industry's input costs and customer markets can help identify areas for further company research. Instead of drawing a fixed supply-chain diagram, the app lets me investigate the size of relationships and whether their importance has changed.

These are economy-wide industry relationships, not verified links between individual companies. The views cover intermediate production inputs, including imported inputs, and exclude final uses such as capital investment, household consumption and exports. Dollar changes reflect both prices and quantities; they do not predict stock returns.

## How it works

A Python/FastAPI backend reads the official workbook, checks its integrity and published totals, and calculates rankings, shares and historical changes. The web interface uses HTML, CSS and JavaScript to draw the charts and handle selections. The source snapshot is included with the app, so exploring it requires no API key or live data connection.

**Data source:** [BEA Input-Output Accounts](https://www.bea.gov/data/industries/input-output-accounts-data)
