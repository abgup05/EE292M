Know what is measured

U.S. industry aggregates, including imported inputs. Excludes capital investment, household purchases and exports from these views. It is not a company supplier map.

Read changes carefully

Current-dollar values mix price and quantity changes. “Share” uses the published intermediate total. Negative adjustments are retained; BEA ellipses are unreported, not plotted as zero.
