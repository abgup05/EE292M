    How a run works

    1. Enter a ticker, the decision you face, and the claims your thesis rests on.
    2. The backend reads the company's latest annual report (10-K) and the quarterly reports (10-Q) filed after it, and finds the passages that bear on each claim.
    3. Each claim gets a verdict computed from quotes that were checked word for word against the filing. Open any verdict to read the passage in place.

                  Terms used on this page
                  10-K
                  The annual report a US public company files with the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC).
                  10-Q
                  The quarterly report, filed for each of the other three quarters.
                  Item
                  A numbered section of a filing, such as Item 1A, Risk Factors, or Item 7, Management's Discussion and Analysis (MD&A).
                  Period end
                  The last day of the quarter or fiscal year the filing reports on. Fiscal years need not match calendar years.
                  Filed
                  The date the company submitted the filing to the SEC. "Retrieved" is when this run downloaded it, in UTC.
                  Accession number
                  The SEC's identifier for one filing; hover over a form to see it.
                  CIK
                  The SEC's numeric identifier for a company.
                  EDGAR
                  The SEC's public filing system, where the filings linked on this page are published.
